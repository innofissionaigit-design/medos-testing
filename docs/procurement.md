# Procurement to consumption

## Purpose
Follows stock from "we are running short" to "it was used and what it cost": stock requirement → indent → approval → purchase order → goods receipt with a quantity and quality check → allocation to the wards and the OT → issue and use → cost posted. Every step is a record with who and when, the order of steps is enforced by the application and by the database, and nothing is ever deleted.

## The steps
1. **Stock requirement** (`GET /procurement/requirement`). Medicines whose stock (the main store plus every location) has fallen to the reorder level set on the medicine, counting what is already on a purchase order or on an indent not yet ordered. The suggestion tops the position up to twice the reorder level. Read-only.
2. **Indent.** The pharmacy, a ward or the OT raises an indent: the medicines and quantities needed, and where they are needed (a store, or the main store). It is a draft the requester can edit, then **submitted**.
3. **Approval.** Someone with purchase-approval rights approves or rejects (a rejection needs a reason). When an indent is submitted its **estimated value** is worked out (each line at the latest price this hospital paid for the medicine). Above `INDENT_SECOND_APPROVAL_LIMIT` (0 = never) it needs **two different approvers**: the first approval waits on the indent (`INDENT_FIRST_APPROVED`, it stays submitted) and a second person decides it. The requester can approve neither, the same person cannot give both, and the number of approvals needed is fixed at submission whatever the limit becomes later (database checks hold all of this). **The person who raised an indent cannot approve it**: the service refuses (`SELF_APPROVAL`) and the database refuses as well. Procurement, who place the orders, do not approve.
4. **Purchase order.** Procurement turns an approved indent into purchase orders (one or several, to one or several suppliers), each line within what the indent still needs, with the rate per unit and GST. A part order leaves the rest of the indent open; when every line is on an order the indent is **ordered**. A hospital can also place an order with no indent. **Issuing** an order fixes its lines and totals. Above the hospital's **approval limit** (setting `PO_APPROVAL_LIMIT`, 0 = none) an order must be issued by a person with approval rights who is not the person who made it. A second limit (setting `PO_SECOND_APPROVAL_LIMIT`, 0 = none) adds a second level: above it the order first needs a **first approval** (`POST /procurement/orders/:id/approve`, `PURCHASE_APPROVE`, not the maker), and a different person with approval rights then issues it; the database holds all three people apart. An order can be cancelled until anything has arrived (the quantities go back to the indent) or closed short with a reason when the supplier cannot finish it.
5. **Goods receipt** (Pharmacy → Purchases → Receive purchase; `POST /pharmacy/purchases`). Against an issued order: the supplier must be the order's, each line must be on the order, the price may not exceed the order's rate (`PRICE_ABOVE_ORDER`), and what is accepted may not exceed what is still expected (`OVER_RECEIPT`). A delivery with no order is still received unless the hospital sets `PURCHASE_REQUIRE_PO` (then `PURCHASE_ORDER_REQUIRED`).
6. **Quantity and quality check.** On each line, `quantity` is what the check **accepted into stock**, and `rejectedQuantity` with its `rejectReason` (5+ characters) is what was turned away (damaged, short-dated, wrong item). Only accepted units enter stock and the bill is for the accepted units; the rejection is on the record and audited (`PURCHASE_GOODS_REJECTED`). The order's received quantity counts accepted units only, so a replacement for rejected goods is received later against what is still expected. An order becomes **partially received**, then **received**.
7. **Allocation.** The main store moves stock to ward and OT stores with stock transfers (docs/stock-transfers.md), immediate or in two steps.
8. **Issue and use.** Nurses draw medicines for admitted patients from a ward store, which bills the admission (existing). The OT and departments record **consumption** (`POST /procurement/consumption`): stock used for a patient's procedure (a procedure of this facility that is planned or performed and not yet finalised), or for the department's own use (with a reason). It leaves the location's ledger. Needs an `Idempotency-Key`; an expired or blocked batch cannot be used. For a procedure, `billToPatient: true` also charges the patient's admission at the medicine's selling price (a MEDICINE charge linked to the consumption, `chargeId`); it is refused once the final bill is made, and it is not allowed for department use.
9. **Cost posting.** Each consumption keeps the batch's purchase price (before GST) as its unit and total cost. The report **Stock received, used and held (at cost)** (`GET /reports/pharmacy/consumption-cost`, `REPORT_PHARMACY`/`REPORT_READ`/`REPORT_FINANCE`, ranges up to 93 days) shows: received value (accepted units) and units rejected; the cost used up by sales (net of patient returns), ward issues, procedures and department use; the total cost of consumption; stock written off as damaged, stock added or removed by adjustments (opening stock counts as added) and returns to suppliers; the value of stock still held in the main store and in the locations; consumption by location; and the cost per procedure. Every figure is units times the batch's purchase price read from the stock ledgers, so received − used − written off − returned = stock held, within the range's own movements.

10. **Store requests** (`/procurement/stock-requests`). A ward, the OT or a department asks the main store for stock (medicine and quantity per line, a reason). The store (`PHARMACY_ADJUST_STOCK`) issues it as a stock transfer for chosen batches (never more than asked; `OVER_RECEIPT`), in whole or in part (`PARTIALLY_ISSUED`, then `ISSUED`), or turns it down with a reason; the requester can cancel until anything is issued.
11. **Rejected deliveries and supplier credit.** A goods receipt can carry `rejections` (medicine, units, invoice rate, reason): lines turned away in full, which never enter stock, so a delivery can be rejected entirely (`items: []`). For any rejected goods (part or whole) a **debit note** (`SDN-`) is raised for their value including GST, asking the supplier for credit. When the supplier agrees, the debit note is marked **credited** with the supplier's reference (`POST /procurement/debit-notes/:id/credit`).
12. **Paying suppliers** (`/procurement/payables`, Procurement → Supplier payments, `SUPPLIER_PAY`). Each goods receipt is an amount owing: invoice total less returns to the supplier and credited debit notes, less payments. Payments (cash, UPI, card, bank transfer, other; part payments and several per invoice) never exceed what is owing (service and database), need an `Idempotency-Key`, and are never edited. The list shows what each supplier is owed and what is overdue.
13. **Stock counts** (`/procurement/stock-counts`, `STOCK_COUNT` to count, `STOCK_COUNT_APPROVE` to approve). A count of the main store or one location takes a snapshot of every batch; counters enter what they find (a reason of 5+ characters wherever it differs); when all lines are counted the count is submitted. **Someone other than the person who started it approves**, and the differences are posted as stock adjustments (main store, ledger type ADJUSTMENT) or as location ledger entries; it works by the difference, so sales during the count are not lost. If the stock has moved so far that a removal is no longer possible the approval is refused and the count is cancelled and redone. One open count per place.
14. **Printing.** Purchase order, goods receipt and debit note print from the screens (`/print/purchase-orders/:id`, `/print/goods-receipts/:id`, `/print/debit-notes/:id`), audited like other prints.

15. **Paying a supplier's statement** (`POST /procurement/payables/statement-payments`, Procurement → Supplier payments → Pay a statement, `SUPPLIER_PAY`). One payment settles several invoices of one supplier: either an amount for each invoice, or a single amount spread over the oldest invoices first (by the supplier's invoice date). It is refused (`PAYMENT_EXCEEDS_BALANCE`) if an invoice is not that supplier's open invoice or an amount is more than an invoice leaves owing, and a single amount may not exceed what the supplier is owed in all. The payments are recorded against each invoice under one payment run (the database checks that they add up to the run's total and belong to the supplier), need an `Idempotency-Key`, and are never edited. Audit `SUPPLIER_STATEMENT_PAID`.
16. **Cost ledger** (`GET /procurement/cost-ledger`, `/procurement/cost-ledger/entries`, Procurement → Cost ledger; `REPORT_FINANCE`, `PURCHASE_READ` or `SUPPLIER_PAY`). Each stock and supplier event is posted in the same transaction as a balanced entry (debits equal credits, checked by the database at commit; entries are never edited or deleted): a goods receipt (stock at its price before GST, GST input, owed to the supplier), a return to a supplier, a supplier credit on a debit note, a supplier payment or statement payment (cash or bank), consumption by the OT or a department (cost of stock used, by cost centre, out of stock), **medicines sold to patients** (cost of goods sold at the batch's purchase price, out of stock) and the unit a patient returns (the reverse), **medicines issued to a patient from a ward store**, and **stock adjustments**: opening stock, damage and write-offs, adjustments and stock-count differences in the main store or a location (a gain or loss against the stock adjustment account). Transfers between places move no value, so they post nothing. The trial balance for a period shows each account's opening, debits, credits and closing, the cost of stock used by place, and whether it balances. It is a cost ledger, not general accounts: no revenue, no patient billing, no tax returns. **Reconciliation** (`GET /procurement/cost-ledger/reconciliation`): the stock held at each batch's purchase price (main store plus every location) is compared with what the ledger says is in stock; they agree once everything is posted, and the screen says so or shows the difference. **Back-fill** (`GET|POST /procurement/cost-ledger/backfill`, `SUPPLIER_PAY` or `ADMIN_SETTINGS`, Procurement → Cost ledger → Post earlier history): events from before the ledger existed (receipts, supplier returns and credits, supplier payments, consumption, sales, ward issues, adjustments and counts) are posted from their own records with the same entries and keys as live posting, dated when they happened, in chunks until done; running it again posts nothing (`COST_LEDGER_BACKFILLED`).

## Permissions
`STORES_INDENT` (raise and manage your own indents): Pharmacist, Nurse, OT coordinator, Procurement, Administrator. `PURCHASE_APPROVE` (approve or reject indents, issue large orders): Manager, Administrator. `PURCHASE_ORDER` (place, issue, cancel and close orders): Procurement, Administrator. `PURCHASE_READ` (read everything): Pharmacist, Procurement, Manager, Administrator. `STOCK_CONSUME` (record consumption): OT coordinator, Pharmacist. `PHARMACY_PURCHASE` still receives goods (Pharmacist, Procurement). `SUPPLIER_PAY` (Manager, Administrator) pays suppliers and credits debit notes. `STOCK_COUNT` (Pharmacist, Nurse, OT coordinator, Administrator) counts stock; `STOCK_COUNT_APPROVE` (Manager, Administrator) approves a count. Migration `20261113090000_procurement_completion` adds them. New role **PROCUREMENT** (demo user `procurement1`). Migration `20261112140000_procurement_to_consumption` adds them to existing hospitals.

## Audit events
`INDENT_CREATED/UPDATED/SUBMITTED/APPROVED/REJECTED/CANCELLED`, `PURCHASE_ORDER_CREATED/ISSUED/CANCELLED/CLOSED`, `PURCHASE_RECEIVED`, `PURCHASE_GOODS_REJECTED`, `STOCK_CONSUMED`, `STOCK_REQUEST_CREATED/ISSUED/REJECTED/CANCELLED`, `DEBIT_NOTE_RAISED/CREDITED`, `SUPPLIER_PAID`, `STOCK_COUNT_STARTED/SUBMITTED/APPROVED/CANCELLED`, `PURCHASE_ORDER_APPROVED`, `INDENT_FIRST_APPROVED`, `SUPPLIER_STATEMENT_PAID`.

## API
`GET /procurement/requirement`; `GET|POST /procurement/indents`, `GET|PUT /procurement/indents/:id`, `POST /procurement/indents/:id/submit|approve|reject|cancel`; `GET|POST /procurement/orders`, `GET /procurement/orders/:id`, `POST /procurement/orders/:id/issue|cancel|close`; `POST /pharmacy/purchases` (now with `purchaseOrderId`, `poItemId`, `rejectedQuantity`, `rejectReason`); `GET|POST /procurement/consumption`, `GET /procurement/consumption/procedures`; `GET /reports/pharmacy/consumption-cost`. Also `GET|POST /procurement/stock-requests`, `…/:id/issue|reject|cancel`; `GET /procurement/payables`, `POST /procurement/payables/:purchaseId/payments`; `GET /procurement/debit-notes`, `POST …/:id/credit`; `GET|POST /procurement/stock-counts`, `…/:id/counts|submit|approve|cancel`; `POST /procurement/orders/:id/approve`. Settings: `PURCHASE_REQUIRE_PO`, `PO_APPROVAL_LIMIT`, `PO_SECOND_APPROVAL_LIMIT`, `INDENT_SECOND_APPROVAL_LIMIT`. Screens: the **Procurement** page and Pharmacy → Purchases.

## Database rules
Indents and orders are never deleted. An indent's lines are changed only while it is a draft; an issued order's lines and totals never change (only what has been received moves, upwards). Status changes follow the paths above. An indent's decider is never its requester; an approved order's approver is never its maker. A rejection has a reason. Consumption records are immutable and the location ledger records exactly one cause (a transfer, a ward issue or a consumption) per movement.

## Known limitations
Indents and orders have up to two levels of approval, by amount. The cost ledger is not a general ledger (no revenue, patient billing or tax returns); earlier history is posted only when someone runs the back-fill, and the cost of a unit is the batch's purchase price, so a batch whose price was never recorded (zero) posts nothing. Cost is the batch's purchase price before GST. A statement payment is made against invoices MedOS holds as goods receipts; it does not read the supplier's own statement. Tests: `procurement/procurement.spec.ts`, `procurement/procurement-completion.spec.ts`, `procurement/procurement-finishing.spec.ts`, `procurement/cost-ledger.spec.ts`.
