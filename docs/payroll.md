# Payroll

## Purpose
Monthly salary for the hospital's employees: pay structures, a monthly run that makes one payslip per employee, approval by a second person, payment, and payslips the employee can print. Doctor commission and fixed fees are separate (docs/doctor-payouts.md). Daily attendance and leave feed the monthly run (see below). The rest of HR (recruitment, appraisals) is not part of this module.

## Entities
- **Employee**: code (`EMP-0001`), name, designation, department, joining and last day, optional link to a user (so they can see their own payslips), bank account, IFSC, PAN, UAN, ESI number. Bank account and PAN are shown masked. Never deleted; made inactive instead.
- **Salary structure**: basic, house rent allowance, other allowance, and whether provident fund, ESI and professional tax apply, from a date. Append-only: a raise is a new structure from a later date. A payslip uses the latest structure that started on or before the month's last day.
- **Payroll run**: one per facility per month. States `DRAFT → APPROVED → PAID`; a draft can be `CANCELLED` (with a reason), which frees the month.
- **Payslip**: one per employee per run, with every figure calculated by the server.

## Workflow
1. The payroll officer adds employees and sets their pay.
2. At month end, the officer starts the month's payroll. Every active employee who was employed in the month and has a salary structure gets a payslip; anyone without one is listed as left out. Joining or leaving mid-month, and absences, half days and unpaid leave from the attendance sheet, are counted as days of loss of pay automatically; the screen lists employees whose days were not all marked.
3. On each draft payslip the officer enters days of loss of pay (half days allowed), bonus, income tax (TDS) and other deductions. Provident fund, ESI and professional tax are recalculated each time.
4. A different person with `PAYROLL_APPROVE` (the administrator) approves. This fixes every figure and the run's totals. The person who prepared the run cannot approve it (checked in the service and again in the database).
5. The approver marks the run paid with the date and the bank batch reference.
6. Employees see their own payslips on My account once the run is approved, and can print them.

## Calculation (defaults, to be confirmed by the hospital's accountant)
The rates below are the **starting values** of the hospital setting `PAYROLL_STATUTORY_RATES` (Administration → Settings → Payroll). The accountant changes them there; no code change is needed. They have **not** been checked against current law, and that check cannot be done by the software.

Each pay component is scaled by days worked ÷ days in the month, rounded to the paisa. Provident fund: 12% of basic up to a ₹15,000 ceiling (employee and employer), rounded to the rupee. ESI: employee 0.75% and employer 3.25% of gross when gross is ₹21,000 or less, rounded up to the rupee. Professional tax: West Bengal slabs by monthly gross (up to ₹10,000 nil, ₹110, ₹130, ₹150, then ₹200). TDS is entered by hand. The defaults live in `payroll.domain.ts`. A change to the setting applies to payslips calculated or edited afterwards; an approved run never changes. **Arrears** (below) are added to gross after these amounts are worked out on the month's regular pay: they do not change provident fund, ESI or professional tax. Whether that is right for the hospital is for the accountant to confirm.

## Permissions
`PAYROLL_READ` (see employees, runs, payslips, attendance, leave), `PAYROLL_MANAGE` (employees, pay, drafts, cancel, leave types), `PAYROLL_APPROVE` (approve, mark paid), `ATTENDANCE_MANAGE` (mark attendance, enter or cancel leave for anyone), `LEAVE_APPROVE` (approve or reject leave). Roles: `PAYROLL_OFFICER` has read, manage and attendance; `ADMINISTRATOR` has read, approve and leave approval. Own payslips need no permission.

## Audit events
`PAYROLL_EMPLOYEE_ADDED/UPDATED`, `ATTENDANCE_MARKED`, `ATTENDANCE_CORRECTED`, `ATTENDANCE_IMPORTED`, `PAYROLL_HOLIDAY_ADDED/REMOVED`, `PAYROLL_ARREARS_ADDED`, `LEAVE_TYPE_CREATED/UPDATED`, `LEAVE_REQUESTED`, `LEAVE_APPROVED`, `LEAVE_REJECTED`, `LEAVE_CANCELLED`, `PAYROLL_STRUCTURE_SET`, `PAYROLL_RUN_CREATED`, `PAYROLL_SLIP_ADJUSTED`, `PAYROLL_RUN_APPROVED`, `PAYROLL_RUN_PAID`, `PAYROLL_RUN_CANCELLED`, `DOCUMENT_PRINTED`.

## Database rules
Employees, structures and runs are never deleted; structures cannot be edited. A payslip must add up (gross, deductions, net) and use a structure of the same employee. After approval neither the run's figures nor its payslips can change; a paid or cancelled run is frozen. A run can only be paid after approval, and not approved by its preparer. One live run per facility and month.

## API
`GET/POST /payroll/employees`, `PATCH /payroll/employees/:id`, `GET/POST /payroll/employees/:id/structures`, `GET/POST /payroll/runs`, `GET /payroll/runs/:id`, `PUT /payroll/runs/:id/payslips/:slipId`, `POST /payroll/runs/:id/approve|pay|cancel`, `GET /payroll/my-payslips`, `GET /print/payslips/:id`.

## Attendance and leave
- **Attendance** is marked a day at a time on Payroll → Attendance: present, absent, half day, weekly off or holiday (leave days come from approved leave). Re-marking a day corrects it and each correction is audited (`ATTENDANCE_CORRECTED`, with the old and new mark). Presence cannot be marked for a day that has not come; a day outside the employee's time at the hospital is refused. The month sheet shows the counts, the days not marked and the days of loss of pay that follow.
- **Days of loss of pay** = a full absence (1) + a half day (0.5) + each day of unpaid leave (1). Paid leave, weekly offs, holidays and presence cost nothing. Days nobody marked are **not** charged; starting the month's run lists who has unmarked days. These are added to the days outside the employee's time at the hospital (joining or leaving mid-month), never above the days in the month. The payslip note says "From attendance: ..."; the officer can still edit the days on a draft payslip.
- **Weekly offs and holidays:** the weekly off days are a setting (`PAYROLL_WEEKLY_OFF_DAYS`, empty by default: no common weekly off is assumed) and public holidays are a calendar (Payroll → Leave → Holidays). Neither needs marking, neither is charged as loss of pay, neither counts as unmarked, and leave skips them. A person marked present on a holiday keeps that mark. Holidays are never edited (remove and add again), and adding or removing one in a month that has a live payroll run is refused.
- **Time-clock import:** on Payroll → Attendance, paste lines of employee code, date, first in, last out. The officer chooses the hours that make a full day and a half day; the hours between first in and last out (a shift past midnight counts to the next morning) decide present, half day or absent. A preview shows each line before anything is written. A line is rejected with its reason (unknown code, outside employment, a day to come, a month already in payroll, same person and day twice, only one punch) and never guessed at; days of approved leave and days already marked the same way are left alone. Applying is audited (`ATTENDANCE_IMPORTED`, and `ATTENDANCE_CORRECTED` for each changed mark).
- **Leave types** are the hospital's own: a name, paid or unpaid, a yearly allowance (blank = no limit), and how many unused days carry forward. "Add the usual leave types" creates Casual 12, Sick 12, Earned 15 and Leave without pay as a starting point only; change them to the hospital's leave policy. A type that has been used cannot change between paid and unpaid.
- **Carry-forward:** for a leave type with a yearly allowance and a carry-forward limit, an employee who joined before the year began starts the year with the unused part of last year's own allowance (not last year's carried days again), up to the limit. It is worked out when asked from approved leave, using the type's current allowance; it is not prorated for someone who joined part-way through last year.
- **Half-day leave:** an application for a single day can be for half a day. Approving marks the day as half leave (the other half is worked), counts 0.5 against the balance, and costs half a day of pay only for an unpaid type. Whether it is a half day cannot change after applying.
- **Applying and deciding:** an employee whose login is linked to their employee record applies on My account (or the payroll officer enters it). Nothing changes in attendance until it is approved. Approving needs `LEAVE_APPROVE`, writes an "on leave" mark for each day in the range except days already marked weekly off or holiday (those are not counted), and checks the year's balance. Nobody can approve or reject leave they asked for or that is their own. A rejection needs a reason the employee can read. Approved or waiting leave can be cancelled; cancelling approved leave leaves its days unmarked. Leave must fall within one calendar year and within the employee's time at the hospital, and cannot overlap other waiting or approved leave.
- **A month with a payroll run is frozen.** While a run for the month exists that is not cancelled, attendance for it cannot be marked or changed and leave touching it cannot be applied, approved or cancelled (`PAYROLL_MONTH_LOCKED`; also enforced by the database). To correct it, cancel the draft run, fix the sheet and start the run again. Marking and starting a run take the same lock, so a payslip always agrees with the sheet it was made from.
- **Database rules:** leave types and applications are never deleted; an application's person, dates and type never change; it is decided once and an approved one can only be cancelled; the decider differs from the requester; a leave day must belong to an application and only leave days do.
- **API:** `GET/PUT /payroll/attendance`, `GET/POST /payroll/leave-types`, `POST /payroll/attendance/import`, `GET/POST /payroll/holidays`, `POST /payroll/holidays/:id/remove`, `GET/POST /payroll/runs/:id/payslips/:slipId/arrears`, `POST /payroll/leave-types/standard`, `PATCH /payroll/leave-types/:id`, `GET/POST /payroll/leave-requests`, `POST /payroll/leave-requests/:id/approve|reject|cancel`, `GET /payroll/employees/:id/leave-balance`, and the employee's own `GET/POST /payroll/my-leave`, `POST /payroll/my-leave/:id/cancel`.

## Arrears
Pay owed for earlier months, paid on a later payslip. On a draft payslip the officer sees **Pay owed for earlier months**: for each approved or paid month of the last year, what the salary structure that applies to that month today would have paid for the days worked, less what the payslip paid (a backdated raise is the usual case). Only a positive difference is suggested. The officer picks the months to add; each earlier payslip can be added once (a record ties it to the payslip that took it, and a cancelled run frees it again). Arrears can also be typed by hand with a note. They show on the payslip and its print, count in gross and in the run's totals, and are audited (`PAYROLL_ARREARS_ADDED`). Recovery of an overpayment (a negative difference) is not suggested: enter it as an other deduction.

## Known limitations
Comp-off, leave across two years in one application, shift rosters, unpaid-leave carry rules and proration of carry-forward for part-year joiners are not built. Leave in a month whose payroll is already approved cannot be corrected by cancelling a run: the difference is paid as arrears in a later month. Nothing here has been checked against labour or tax law. No advances or loan recovery schedules; no Form 16 or TDS computation; no PF/ESI challan or bank file generation; no multiple salary heads beyond basic, HRA and one other allowance; one currency (INR). See `docs/backlog.md`.
