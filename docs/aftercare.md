# Post-discharge patient journey

## Purpose
Follows a patient after a stay: **discharge → review due → call centre → appointment → review consultation → ongoing record → feedback and complaints**. Nothing here is a separate scheduling system: the review visit is an ordinary `FOLLOW_UP` appointment (docs/follow-ups.md) linked to the stay's encounter, so the doctor's schedule, check-in, the OPD queue, the appointment reminders and the patient's record all apply.

## The steps
1. **Discharge makes the review task.** When the doctor completes a discharge (`POST /admissions/:id/discharge/complete`) an **aftercare task** is made in the same transaction, one per stay. The due date is the signed discharge summary's follow-up advice (a date, or a number of days from discharge) and its note; with no advice the hospital's `AFTERCARE_REVIEW_DAYS` (default 7) is used. No task is made after a death or an absconding.
2. **Call list.** Care coordinators and the call centre (`AFTERCARE_MANAGE`) see **Calls due**: pending reviews due within `AFTERCARE_CALL_LEAD_DAYS` (default 3) of today or already late, plus patients whose planned call-back time has come. Other views: all pending, booked, seen, closed; "mine"; search by name, mobile or MRN.
3. **A call** (`POST /aftercare/tasks/:id/contacts`) records the outcome: no answer, call again at a time, wrong number, does not want a review (closes the task, reason required), already seen elsewhere (closes it, reason required). Calls are never edited. The first person to act on a task takes it; it can be handed to a colleague (`PATCH /aftercare/tasks/:id`), and the due date can be moved.
4. **Booking** (`POST /aftercare/tasks/:id/book`) books the review as a follow-up appointment with a doctor, in the same transaction as the task change. All appointment rules apply (doctor free, patient not double-booked, working hours). The task becomes **booked**.
5. **The visit.** The task follows the appointment, in the database: when the review visit is **completed** the task is **completed** (seen); when the appointment is **cancelled or missed** the task goes back to **pending** and the patient is due a call again at once. A booked review cannot be closed by staff: cancel the visit first.
6. **Ongoing record.** The review is an OPD consultation linked to the stay: it appears in the patient's history with the admission, and may itself order a further follow-up (docs/follow-ups.md).
7. **Feedback** (`POST /aftercare/feedback`): a rating 1 to 5, a comment, and how it was taken (phone, in person, form). From a review task the patient and stay come from the task. Reception, nurses, coordinators and patient relations can record it; coordinators and patient relations read it (`GET /aftercare/feedback`, low ratings filter). Feedback is never edited.
8. **Complaints** (`/complaints`): raised from a call, a feedback entry or the desk (category, severity, what happened). Patient relations (`COMPLAINT_MANAGE`) work them: **take up** → note → **resolve** (a written outcome of 10+ characters) → patient told → **close**; a resolved complaint can be **reopened** with a reason; a closed one is final (raise a new one). Hand-over to another person is recorded. Every step is in the complaint's history with who and when, and what was complained about is never changed.
9. **Summary** (`GET /aftercare/summary`, default last 30 days): reviews due, reached, booked, seen, closed; average rating and counts per rating; complaints raised, open, resolved and average days to resolve.

## State transitions
Review task: `PENDING → BOOKED | CLOSED`, `BOOKED → COMPLETED | PENDING | CLOSED`; completed and closed are final. Complaint: `OPEN → IN_PROGRESS | RESOLVED`, `IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED`, `RESOLVED → CLOSED | IN_PROGRESS`; closed is final. Enforced in the service and by database triggers.

## Permissions
`AFTERCARE_MANAGE` (call list, calls, booking, summary): Care coordinator, Manager, Administrator. `FEEDBACK_RECORD` (record feedback, raise a complaint): Receptionist, Nurse, Care coordinator, Patient relations, Administrator. `COMPLAINT_READ` / `COMPLAINT_MANAGE` (read and work complaints, read feedback): Patient relations, Manager, Administrator. New roles **CARE_COORDINATOR** (demo user `coordinator1`) and **PATIENT_RELATIONS** (`relations1`). Migration `20261114090000_aftercare` adds them to existing hospitals.

## Audit events
`AFTERCARE_TASK_CREATED`, `AFTERCARE_CONTACTED`, `AFTERCARE_BOOKED`, `AFTERCARE_UPDATED`, `AFTERCARE_CLOSED`, `FEEDBACK_RECORDED`, `COMPLAINT_RAISED/ASSIGNED/STARTED/RESOLVED/CLOSED/REOPENED/ESCALATED`, `FEEDBACK_LINK_ISSUED`.

## Data
`AftercareTask` (one per admission), `ContactAttempt`, `PatientFeedback`, `FeedbackRequest`, `Complaint`, `ComplaintNote`; all carry `tenantId` and are under row-level security. Tasks and complaints are never deleted; calls, feedback and complaint history are immutable. Complaint numbers `CMP-{YEAR}-{SEQ}` come from the numbering engine. Settings: `AFTERCARE_REVIEW_DAYS`, `AFTERCARE_CALL_LEAD_DAYS`. Screen: **After discharge**.

## The call queue
**Take the next patient** (`POST /aftercare/queue/next`, `AFTERCARE_MANAGE`) gives the caller the most overdue review that is due for a call and that nobody else is on, and holds it for them for 15 minutes; they are the only person offered it until then (asking again returns the same patient). Two people asking at once get two different patients (rows are claimed with `SKIP LOCKED`). Logging the call or booking the review ends the claim; **Release** (`POST /aftercare/tasks/:id/release`) hands the patient back without a call; an unanswered claim times out. A call-back time keeps a patient out of the queue until it arrives. The call list shows who is on a call. Audit `AFTERCARE_CLAIMED`, `AFTERCARE_RELEASED`.

## Call scripts and daily targets
**Scripts** (`GET /aftercare/scripts`, `PUT /aftercare/scripts/:kind`, `POST /aftercare/scripts/:kind/reset`; reading needs `AFTERCARE_MANAGE`, writing `AFTERCARE_CONFIGURE`: Manager and Administrator): what a caller says on a first call, a call-back, when booking, when a patient does not want a review, and when asking for feedback. Built-in wording is used until a hospital writes its own; `{patient}`, `{doctor}`, `{due}` and `{hospital}` are filled in for each patient (any other word in braces is refused), and a hospital can go back to the built-in wording. In the call window, `GET /aftercare/tasks/:id/script` gives the script for that patient (the first-call script before any call, the call-back script after). Audit `AFTERCARE_SCRIPT_SAVED/RESET`.

**Targets** (`GET /aftercare/targets`): today's calls, answered calls and bookings for the caller against `AFTERCARE_DAILY_CALL_TARGET` (default 30) and `AFTERCARE_DAILY_BOOKING_TARGET` (default 5), 0 meaning no target, counted in the hospital's own day; people with `AFTERCARE_CONFIGURE` also see every caller's figures. A call counts as answered unless it was no answer or a wrong number. The call list shows the bars.

## Automatic texts, calls, the patient's own form, complaint deadlines
- **Text before the review** (`AftercareJobsService`, polled like the appointment reminders): a patient whose review is due within `AFTERCARE_CALL_LEAD_DAYS` and is not booked gets one text (hospital name and date only, no clinical detail), if they have a mobile number and have not refused messages. A gateway failure is retried at 5 and 10 minutes; after three tries it stops and the call list remains the reminder. Setting `AFTERCARE_REMINDER_ENABLED`.
- **Calls:** *Call* opens the phone or softphone on the staff member's device with the patient's number (`tel:`) and starts a timer; *End call* stops it and the length is saved with the outcome. There is no PBX or cloud-telephony connection, so a call is placed by the staff member's own phone or softphone.
- **The patient's feedback form:** staff make a link (`POST /aftercare/tasks/:id/feedback-link`, optionally texted); the worker also texts one after a review visit is completed (`AFTERCARE_FEEDBACK_REQUEST_ENABLED`, needs `PUBLIC_WEB_URL`). The patient opens `/feedback/<link>` with no sign-in, gives 1 to 5, a comment and, if they wish, a complaint. The link is random, only its hash is stored, it works once for 14 days, and a wrong, expired or used link all answer the same; the page shows nothing about the patient. A patient's complaint is recorded as MEDIUM and says it came through the link.
- **Complaint deadlines:** each complaint gets a deadline from its severity (`COMPLAINT_HOURS_HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW`, default 24/72/168 hours). One not resolved by then is **escalated** by the worker to the hospital's first active Manager (or Administrator): a note in its history, an audit event (`COMPLAINT_ESCALATED`), and it shows on that person's list ("mine"). Patient relations can also send one up by hand with a reason (`POST /complaints/:id/escalate`), once, while it is open. The list can show only the overdue ones.

## Known limitations
No telephony beyond click-to-call and the call timer: no audio recording and no PBX or cloud-telephony connection (that needs a provider account). The texts go through the same gateway as appointment reminders: with the log-only provider nothing is actually sent. Escalation is one level, to a manager; there is no further escalation or repeat reminder. Tests: `aftercare/aftercare.spec.ts`.
