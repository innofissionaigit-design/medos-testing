# Insurance claims

## Purpose
Tracks money owed by insurers, TPAs and government schemes. A claim follows one issued invoice from pre-authorisation to settlement. Money the insurer pays is posted on the invoice, so the invoice and the claim never disagree.

## Entities
- **Insurer**: name and kind (insurer, TPA, government scheme, corporate); can be made inactive, never deleted.
- **Policy**: a patient's policy with an insurer: number, member id, validity dates, sum insured. A claim needs a policy that is active and valid today.
- **Claim**: patient, policy, optional visit, kind (cashless or reimbursement), the amounts, and the status.
- **Insurer check** (payer verification): the last answer the insurer or TPA gave about the patient's cover, with their reference, who recorded it and when.
- **Enhancement request**: a request, while the patient is admitted, for the insurer to authorise more than the first pre-authorisation.
- **Non-payable item**: a charge on the bill the insurer will not pay (description, amount, why). It is left out of the claim and paid by the patient.
- **Appeal**: the hospital's dispute of an insurer's rejection of a submitted claim, answered once: won (the claim is approved again) or lost.
- **Rate card**: what an insurer pays per service (one rate per insurer and service).
- **Package**: a fixed price an insurer pays for a whole treatment (code, name, price).
- **Further invoices**: more invoices of the same patient that one claim covers beside its main invoice.
- **Timeline** and **settlements**: append-only records of every step and every receipt of money.

## Workflow and states
`DRAFT → PREAUTH_PENDING → PREAUTH_APPROVED → SUBMITTED ⇄ QUERY → APPROVED → SETTLED`, with `REJECTED` (the insurer said no) and `CLOSED` (withdrawn or written off, with a reason) as other ends.

1. A claim is started for a patient and a policy. A cashless claim may first ask for pre-authorisation (an amount, then the insurer's approved amount or rejection reason). A reimbursement claim goes straight to submission.
2. Submitting attaches one issued invoice and the amount claimed (never more than the invoice is worth). An invoice can have only one live claim; it is free again when the claim is rejected or closed.
3. The insurer may raise a query; the reply resubmits the claim.
4. The insurer approves (up to what was claimed) or rejects (with their reason).
5. Money received is recorded against an approved claim with the bank reference and date. Each receipt is posted as a bank-transfer payment on the invoice in the same transaction, which also updates the invoice's paid amount and status. Several part payments are allowed; the claim becomes SETTLED when the approved amount has arrived. A receipt cannot exceed what is still approved or what the invoice still owes.
6. **Insurer check.** At any time until the claim is finished, the desk records the result of checking the patient's cover with the insurer or TPA (by call, portal or email): *cover confirmed* or *not eligible*, with the insurer's reference (3+ characters); "not eligible" also needs the reason they gave. A later check replaces the earlier answer on the claim; every check stays on the timeline and in the audit trail. If the last answer is *not eligible*, a pre-authorisation cannot be requested until a new check says otherwise. A check is not required before pre-authorisation, and MedOS does not contact the insurer itself.
7. **Enhancement requests.** On a cashless claim whose pre-authorisation is approved and not yet submitted, the desk can ask for more authorisation (an extra amount and a reason of 10+ characters). One request waits at a time. The insurer's answer is recorded once: approved (for up to what was asked, which is **added** to the pre-authorised amount) or rejected (with their reason, nothing changes). A claim cannot be submitted while a request is waiting. Decided requests are never changed or deleted (checked by the database as well).
8. **Non-payable items.** Before the claim is submitted (or while it is under query) the desk lists charges the insurer will not pay: description, amount (> 0) and why (5+ characters). Together they may not exceed the invoice. They come off the invoice **before** the co-pay is worked out, so the claim never asks for them, and the patient's share is everything not claimed (non-payable items plus deductible and co-pay). Submission and resubmission refuse a claimed amount above what is left after them. A wrong item is removed with a reason (5+ characters); it stays on the record. Once the claim is submitted the list is fixed until a query reopens it.
9. **Appeals.** A claim the insurer rejected after it was submitted (it has its invoice and claimed amount) can be appealed with a reason (10+ characters). One appeal waits at a time. The insurer's answer is recorded once: *won* (the claim becomes approved again for the amount they now allow, up to what was claimed; the rejection reason is cleared and the reversal stays on the timeline; settlement then follows as usual) or *lost* (their reason is kept; the claim stays rejected, and a new appeal may follow). A refused pre-authorisation is not appealed; start a new claim. If the invoice meanwhile has another live claim, winning is refused (`409`). The database allows a rejected claim to be approved again only when an appeal against it was won.
10. **Rate cards and packages.** `CLAIM_MASTERS` sets an insurer's rate per service and its packages (code, name, price; made inactive, never deleted). "Check against the insurer's rates" compares an invoice's lines with the rate card: a line billed above the insurer's rate has its excess (billed minus rate, times quantity, before tax) left to the patient, recorded as a non-payable item (once per line; running it again adds nothing). Lines without a rate are not touched. A claim can be put under one of **its own insurer's** active packages before submission (or under query): it then never asks the insurer for more than the package price, and the rest is the patient's. The claim keeps the price it chose the package at; later package price changes do not touch it.
11. **Several invoices.** A claim is submitted with a main invoice and optionally more issued invoices of the same patient (`extraInvoiceIds`); more can join while the claim is under query (`POST /claims/:id/invoices`). The claimed amount, co-pay, non-payable items and package cap are all worked out on the invoices together. No invoice can be on two live claims. A link is never changed or removed. Money received is posted against the main invoice first, then the others in the order they joined, each up to what it still owes.
12. **TDS.** When the insurer deducts tax at source, the receipt is recorded with the cash received (`amount`) and the tax withheld (`tdsAmount`, 0 or more). Both count towards the approved amount, so the claim settles when cash plus TDS reach it. Each invoice is credited with both: the cash as a bank transfer, the TDS as an "other" payment whose reference starts with `TDS` (it is a tax credit, not cash, and reports list it under other payments). Cash plus TDS cannot exceed the approved amount or what the invoices owe.
13. A claim can be closed with a reason (for example, the balance is written off). Finished claims (rejected, settled, closed) cannot move again.

## Permissions
`CLAIM_READ`, `CLAIM_MANAGE` (policies and the steps), `CLAIM_SETTLE` (record money received), `CLAIM_MASTERS` (insurers). Role: `CLAIMS_OFFICER` has all four. Administrators can read and set up insurers; managers, cashiers and reception can read.

## Audit events
`INSURER_CREATED/UPDATED`, `POLICY_ADDED`, `POLICY_STATUS_CHANGED`, `CLAIM_CREATED`, `CLAIM_PREAUTH_REQUESTED/APPROVED/REJECTED`, `CLAIM_PAYER_VERIFIED`, `CLAIM_NON_PAYABLE_ADDED/REMOVED`, `CLAIM_INVOICE_ADDED`, `CLAIM_PACKAGE_CHOSEN`, `INSURER_RATE_SET`, `INSURER_PACKAGE_ADDED/UPDATED`, `CLAIM_APPEAL_REQUESTED/OVERTURNED/UPHELD`, `CLAIM_ENHANCEMENT_REQUESTED/APPROVED/REJECTED`, `CLAIM_SUBMITTED`, `CLAIM_QUERY_RAISED`, `CLAIM_RESUBMITTED`, `CLAIM_APPROVED`, `CLAIM_REJECTED`, `CLAIM_SETTLEMENT_RECEIVED`, `CLAIM_CLOSED`.

## Database rules
Claims, insurers and policies are never deleted. The timeline and settlements are append-only. Amounts keep their order (approved ≤ claimed; received ≤ approved). A finished claim cannot change status; money received cannot be taken back; a claim cannot move to another patient. One live claim per invoice. Settlements are only accepted on an approved claim and cannot add up to more than approved.

## Documents
Supporting documents (discharge summary, policy card, ID proof, investigation reports, final bill, prescription, or anything else the insurer asks for) are attached to the claim in the claim's window. PNG, JPEG or PDF, up to 5 MB each and 30 per claim, checked against their declared type and virus-scanned like patient documents. The claims officer (`CLAIM_MANAGE`) adds and removes them while the claim is open; anyone with `CLAIM_READ` can open them. Opening a document is audited (`CLAIM_DOCUMENT_VIEWED`) because it is access to a patient's record. A wrong document is removed with a reason (5+ characters): it stops showing but stays on the record, and nothing is ever deleted. A rejected, settled or closed claim keeps its documents and takes no more (checked by the service and by the database). The documents are **kept with the claim; nothing is sent to the insurer**.
API: `POST /claims/:id/documents?filename=&label=` (raw body), `GET /claims/:id/documents`, `GET /claim-documents/:id/content`, `POST /claim-documents/:id/remove`. Audit events: `CLAIM_DOCUMENT_ADDED`, `CLAIM_DOCUMENT_VIEWED`, `CLAIM_DOCUMENT_REMOVED`, `CLAIM_DOCUMENT_REJECTED`. Tests: `claims/claims.spec.ts`.

## Discharge checklist
The claim linked to an admission (`encounterId` of the admission) feeds the **Insurance** stage of the discharge checklist: documents attached, authorisation requested and answered (docs/discharge-stages.md). The Discharge tab has an **Insurance** card to start the claim for the admission and to open the claim window (documents, pre-authorisation). The claims officer role has `ADMISSION_READ` so the TPA desk can open the admission; it still cannot waive or discharge. Migration `20261110090000_discharge_insurance_step` adds the `INSURANCE` clearance step and that permission. Demo seed user: `tpa1`. Tests: `discharge/discharge.spec.ts` ("insurance clearance").

## The whole cashless journey
Payer check, pre-authorisation, treatment, enhancement requests, final bill, non-payable items, final authorisation, the patient's share, discharge, and claim and settlement are covered end to end by one test, **MANDATORY CASHLESS FLOW** (`discharge/discharge.spec.ts`, "cashless insurance journey, end to end"): it follows one admitted patient from the insurer check to a settled claim and a paid bill, and checks each step's audit event and that another hospital cannot see or touch the claim.

**The insurer's share before and after discharge.** Once a claim is submitted, the insurer's expected share on the patient's statement and in the discharge billing check is the amount the claim asks for (after co-pay, non-payable items, any package cap), if that is less than the pre-authorisation; after the insurer's decision it is the approved amount. So the patient is asked to pay their co-pay and the non-payable items before discharge, and the insurer's share is shown as awaited. The Insurance discharge stage then reads "Claim approved by the insurer for X" once the insurer has decided.

## Known limitations
No live eligibility check with the insurer (the desk records what the insurer said; MedOS does not ask them) and no live submission; appeals are recorded by hand like every other insurer answer. No live electronic submission to insurers or TPAs (portals, ABDM or NHCX): it needs each payer's gateway and credentials. Instead, once a claim is submitted with its invoice, the TPA desk downloads the **claim package** (`GET /claims/:id/package`, "Download claim package" on the claim): one structured file (`medos-claim-package/1`) with the claim, patient, policy, co-pay snapshot, the issued invoice and its lines, the list of supporting documents with their SHA-256 checksums, and the timeline. It is uploaded on the insurer's portal by hand, and the insurer's reply is recorded as today. Every export is audited (`CLAIM_PACKAGE_EXPORTED`); the package says `transmitted: false` because MedOS sends nothing; no room-rent cap or per-claim limit (co-pay percentage and deductible only); the deductible already used is entered by hand and is not updated from settled claims; TDS is recorded per payment but not posted to a tax ledger (there is no accounting module); rate cards are per service only (no date ranges); a package covers the whole claim, not part of it. See `docs/backlog.md`.

## Co-pay and deductible
A policy can carry a **co-pay percentage** (what the patient pays of the covered amount), a **deductible** (a fixed amount the patient bears first) and the **deductible already used** this policy year (entered by hand). A policy with neither a percentage nor a deductible has no rule, and the claimed amount is entered by hand as before.

**Calculation** (`calculateCopay`, exact decimals): the deductible still to be met comes off the invoice first (never more than the invoice); the co-pay percentage applies to what is left, rounded to the paisa, half up; the claim is the remainder, never more than the sum insured. The patient's share is whatever the insurer is not asked for, so the two always add up to the invoice (after credit notes).

**At submission** the calculated amount is the default. `POST /claims/:id/copay-preview` (`{ invoiceId }`) shows the breakdown without saving. `submit` may leave `claimedAmount` out to use it; a different amount needs `overrideReason` (10+ characters). The claim keeps a snapshot (calculated amount, patient share, percentage and deductible applied, override reason), so later changes to the policy never alter it. A resubmit that moves the amount away from the calculation also needs a reason. If the patient's share covers the whole bill (`409 INVALID_STATE`) there is nothing to claim.

**Policy rule:** `POST /patients/:id/policies` accepts `copayPercent`, `deductible`, `deductibleUsed`; `PUT /policies/:id/copay` changes or removes it (`copayPercent: null`, deductible 0). Used cannot exceed the deductible. Permission `CLAIM_MANAGE`.

**Audit:** `POLICY_COPAY_CHANGED` (before and after); `CLAIM_SUBMITTED` / `CLAIM_RESUBMITTED` metadata carries the percentage, deductible, calculated amount and any override with its reason.

**Screens:** the policy form has Patient pays %, Deductible and Deductible already used; the submit step shows the breakdown and pre-fills the amount; the printed claim form shows what the patient pays.

**Database:** migration `20261103090000_claims_copay` adds the columns with check constraints (percent 0–100, amounts not negative, override reason at least 10 characters).

## Standard-format exchange with the insurer
A claim can be turned into HL7 FHIR R4 messages (`claim-fhir.ts`, no clinical notes: the diagnosis text on the claim and what was billed): `GET /claims/:id/eligibility-request` (a CoverageEligibilityRequest bundle with the Patient, Coverage, insurer and hospital), and `GET /claims/:id/export?format=fhir|csv[&stage=PREAUTH|CLAIM][&download=1]` (a Claim bundle with the billed lines as items and the claimed amount as total, `use` being `preauthorization` before an invoice exists and `claim` after; or a CSV with one row per billed line for a portal upload). Exports are audited (`CLAIM_EXPORTED`).

`POST /claims/:id/transmit` (`CLAIM_MANAGE`, body `{ kind: "ELIGIBILITY" | "CLAIM" }`) sends the message through the hospital's insurer channel and records the attempt (`GET /claims/:id/transmissions`: kind, status, channel, HTTP status, the insurer's reference, who and when, never the content or the key; the records are never edited). With `CLAIM_TRANSPORT=log` (the default) nothing leaves the hospital: the attempt is recorded as `NOT_SENT` and says so, and the file is downloaded and uploaded to the insurer's portal. With `CLAIM_TRANSPORT=http`, `CLAIM_API_URL` and `CLAIM_API_KEY` (or `CLAIM_API_KEY_FILE`, `CLAIM_TIMEOUT_MS`) the bundle is posted as `application/fhir+json` with a bearer key (the address is checked like every outbound address: no private networks in production, no redirects); a 2xx answer is `SENT` with the insurer's `id`/`identifier` as reference, any other answer or an outage is `FAILED` with the reason. A claim that is settled or closed is not sent. Audit `CLAIM_TRANSMITTED`.

What is **not** done: the insurer's reply is not read from the answer. The eligibility result, the pre-authorisation and claim decisions and the payment are still entered by hand in the claim's steps (verification, decision, settlement), because each insurer answers in its own way and MedOS holds no insurer-specific adapters. Each insurer needs its own address, key and agreement; that cannot be done without them. Tests: `claims/claims.spec.ts` (standard-format exchange).
